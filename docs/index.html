Daily operating procedure

  1. After the NY close, refresh prices and the Friday CFTC COT release.
  2. Recompute each leg's signal on closed bars only. Never intrabar.
  3. Recompute weights; rebalance only if a weight moved more than 2%.
  4. Execute at the next session's open. No chasing, no discretion.
  5. Size each leg to 10% annualised volatility; cap the book at 2× leverage.
  6. Halt for the day if the book loses 3%. Do not re-enter to make it back.
  7. Kill switch: halt and review if drawdown exceeds 12% or the 1-year rolling Sharpe falls below −1.0. Resume after a 60-day cool-off.
  8. Expect to be underwater ~88% of days and to win only 31–38% of trades. The edge arrives through a few long runners — cutting them destroys it.

Known limitations

• The Nasdaq positioning leg fails backward validation on 1999–2009 — it is a post-GFC regime effect, not a market law. Without it the book scores Sharpe 1.17.
• ~40% of returns are generic trend beta available cheaply elsewhere.
• Under the most conservative trial accounting (4,572), the holdout Sharpe does not clear the luck bar. It clears at ~250 independent mechanisms and on the full sample.
• Data is published with a vendor lag; refresh before acting on any signal.
