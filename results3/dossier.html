01 — THE QUESTION

Does any of it beat the market?

The lab began with three families of intraday strategy on ES, NQ, gold and EUR/USD: rule-based (ICT/SMC liquidity sweeps, a technical-vote ensemble), machine-learning (gradient-boosted trees in three weighting schemes), and — added for this study — an AI leg (a neural network). Every model runs through the same discipline: signals from bar-close information only, entries at the next bar's open, stops assumed to fill before targets, spread + slippage + commission on every trade, and an anchored walk-forward where parameters are chosen on training windows the test never touches.

Then we did what the request asked: ensemble the best of all three, add a genuinely new leg, and run a meta-analysis to find real edge with out-of-sample and walk-forward validation. The honest answer is worth more than a flattering one.

05 — THE META-ANALYSIS

Correcting for how many things we tried

Finding a "good" backtest among 115 is trivial — the best of 115 coin-flippers looks brilliant too. Three standard tools price that in:

0.056
White's Reality Check — family-wise p for the single best strategy across all 115. Just misses 0.05 — and the "best" is buy&hold NQ.
0.36
PBO (CSCV) — probability the in-sample leader is below-median out-of-sample. Below ½, but among net losers.
✗
Deflated Sharpe — no strategy's Sharpe clears the deflated benchmark once the trial count is charged against it.

Read together: no genuine, market-beating edge survives. The one strategy that gets within a whisker of significance is buyhold_NQ — i.e. the equity risk premium, which is real but is available to anyone with an index fund. Every attempt to beat that premium — intraday, ML, AI, ensembled, meta-labeled — fails the correction.

06 — WHAT WE LEARNED TRYING TO IMPROVE IT

The discipline earning its keep

Two experiments the request specifically asked for — concentrate and enhance the one promising leg, and hunt for more clues — are where the anti-overfitting machinery mattered most:

  • "Improving" the momentum book made it worse. Four textbook trend refinements (multi-lookback blend, tanh response, 12-1 skip-month, risk-parity + constant-vol) all underperformed the naïve equal-weight baseline (Sharpe 0.35–0.54 vs 0.66). Charged to the trial ledger, the deflation flagged it: the tweaks were fitting noise. We kept the simpler book.
  • The ensemble's only honest move was to stand aside. The "best-of-three, hold cash when nothing has positive trailing edge" policy cut losses ~95% versus always-on (one book: −5% vs −82%), trading 87 times instead of 9,300. It could not manufacture a positive return from negative-expectancy legs — it just correctly refused to trade.
  • No condition rescued the intraday edge. Slicing 479,000 pooled trades by session, hour, day-of-week, volatility, trend and RSI: every bucket was significantly negative. There is no magic hour or regime hiding in it.
  • Lower frequency is where cost stops eating you. The single structural lesson: the intraday ML/AI legs died of turnover, not bad signals. At daily frequency the same momentum idea survives costs — because it trades ~100× less.
07 — WHAT'S ACTUALLY REAL, AND WHAT NEXT

Where this leaves you

Nothing here is a proven money machine — and the framework's value is that it can tell you that instead of selling you a curve fit. What's genuinely supported by the evidence:

  • Risk-premium capture is real (equity + trend/CTA premia), at Sharpe ≈ 0.6–0.8 with 13–17% drawdowns. That's beta with risk control — worth having, not worth pretending it's alpha.
  • The momentum book is a forward-test candidate, not a proven edge (Reality-Check p ≈ 0.34 on its own). The right next step is exactly what's built: run it forward on paper (run_live.py --replay / --watch) and see if the live out-of-sample matches the backtest before risking anything.
  • The unexplored corners that could hold true alpha need data this set doesn't have: options (volatility risk premium), futures term structure (carry), and more instruments for cross-sectional breadth.

Walk-forward "live" here is a paper simulation — no broker, no real orders. This is a research report, not investment advice, and past (even out-of-sample) performance does not guarantee future results.
